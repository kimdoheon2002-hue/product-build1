Learn Korean — Daily 10 Phrases

A free, beginner-friendly way to pick up real-life Korean. Every day we hand-pick 10 practical phrases with romanization, meaning, usage notes, and audio pronunciation — no sign-up required.

Why practice with daily phrases?

Most Korean learners start with long vocabulary lists that are hard to remember because the words are never tied to a real situation. This site takes a different approach: every phrase here is something you might actually say or hear within your first few weeks in Korea — ordering food, asking for directions, thanking someone, or making small talk. Learning ten phrases a day, in context, is far more effective than trying to memorize a hundred isolated words at once.

Each card shows the Korean phrase, how to pronounce it (romanization), what it means in English, and a short note on when native speakers actually use it. Tap the speaker icon to hear it read aloud using your browser's built-in Korean text-to-speech voice, so you can practice your listening and speaking together.

Want to go deeper than daily phrases? Check out our beginner guides on Korean greetings, honorifics, pronunciation, and common mistakes — all written for complete beginners.
