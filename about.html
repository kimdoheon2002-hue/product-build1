About Learn Korean Daily

Learn Korean Daily is a small, free project built for one purpose: to help absolute beginners pick up real, useful Korean without wading through long vocabulary lists or dense grammar textbooks.

What we offer

  • A rotating set of 10 real-life Korean phrases each day, chosen to reflect situations a beginner will actually run into — greetings, dining, shopping, asking for help, and more.
  • Romanization and English meaning for every phrase, so you're never stuck guessing.
  • Audio pronunciation for each phrase, powered by your browser's built-in Korean voice.
  • Free written guides covering greetings, honorifics, pronunciation, and common beginner mistakes — see the Articles section.

Why phrases instead of just vocabulary

Isolated vocabulary lists are easy to forget because they aren't tied to a situation. We believe learning a phrase in context — knowing not just what it means, but when and why a Korean speaker would say it — builds retention and confidence far faster.

Who this is for

This site is aimed at complete beginners: travelers preparing for a trip to Korea, K-drama and K-pop fans curious about the language, or anyone starting their Korean studies from scratch. No sign-up, no account, and no cost — just open the site and start learning.
